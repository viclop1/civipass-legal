Civipass — Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Version française (reference text)

In short

Civipass works without an account, without ads and without analytics tools. Your answers and your progress are stored on your phone; if your phone's backups are turned on, they may be included in them. The only data the app sends directly to Civipass or its providers concerns the purchase of the full version: it is used to verify that purchase and to restore it.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is Víctor López Rodríguez, an independent developer based in Madrid (Spain). For any question about your data: civipassapp@gmail.com.

Data stored locally

The app stores the following locally, on your device:

  • your answers to the questions and your progress by topic;
  • the results of your mock exams;
  • your settings: the procedure you are preparing for (multi-year residence permit, residence card or naturalisation) and the language of the explanations;
  • the fact that you bought the full version, so that the app also works offline;
  • the random identifier used for your purchase (see "Buying the full version").

This information is stored on your phone. Apart from the random identifier, which RevenueCat receives (see below), the app does not send it directly to Civipass or to RevenueCat, and we have no access to it; some of it may, however, be included in backups managed by Apple or Google (see below). The procedure you are preparing for may say something about your administrative status: that is why it is never sent to us. This information is deleted from your phone when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

If you have turned on your phone's backups (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your answers, results and settings may be included in them, like the data of your other apps. Those backups are managed by Apple or Google and linked to your account; we have no access to them. Uninstalling Civipass does not delete backups already made: you can manage them from your Apple or Google account.

The app stores its random identifier and the local purchase confirmation in a file excluded from backups. On iPhone, the RevenueCat module built into the app also keeps a copy of this identifier and the purchase status in the app's internal settings, which may be included in iCloud or computer backups. When the app starts without its own identifier, including after a backup has been restored, it deletes the data stored by RevenueCat before connecting to that service and does not use it again. On a new device, after reinstalling or restoring a backup, the app creates a new identifier. You can recover the full version using the "Restore purchase" button.

Buying the full version

Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on your phone. They are responsible for it under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never receive your name, your email address or your payment details.

To verify the purchase, unlock the full version and restore it on another device, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a provider acting on our behalf as a processor. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created by the app, which does not directly contain your name or contact details;
  • the purchase receipt sent by the store: product, price, currency, date, store country and transaction number;
  • the technical data of the connection: IP address, operating system, app version and device language.

This processing is necessary to perform the purchase contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Apple and Google also send us sales reports (order number, product, price, country and date). We keep them to meet our accounting and tax obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Transfers outside the European Union

RevenueCat processes the data in the United States. This transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914), included in RevenueCat's data processing addendum. Apple and Google may also process your data outside the Union, under their own policies.

How long we keep your data

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • At RevenueCat: for as long as Civipass is offered, because the purchase is permanent and must be restorable at any time, unless you ask for it to be erased (see "Your rights"). When our contract with RevenueCat ends, we will ask it, within 30 days of that end, to return or delete this data, as its data processing addendum provides; that deletion remains subject to its usual backup and archiving practices.
  • Sales reports: we keep the supporting records needed for our accounting and tax obligations for the applicable legal period: six years from the last entry made in the accounting books (Article 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code).

What we do not do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No profiling or automated decisions.
  • The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Civipass is meant for adults preparing for the civic exam.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict its processing or to receive it in a portable format, and, in the cases provided for by the GDPR (Article 21), you can object to its processing. Write to civipassapp@gmail.com.

We do not know who you are. The easiest way for us to find the data that concerns you is the order number shown on the Apple or Google receipt; you can also give us any other information that identifies it (Articles 11 and 12(6) GDPR).

We reply within one month of receiving your request. If the request is complex or we receive many, this period can be extended by two months; we will tell you so before the first month ends (Article 12(3) GDPR).

Erasure. You can ask for erasure at any time. So that no new record is created while we handle your request, we recommend uninstalling Civipass before writing to us and not using it again until we have confirmed the erasure: while it is installed, it keeps the random identifier on your phone and may create a new record at RevenueCat the next time it opens. A backup restored later may contain a copy of the identifier kept by the RevenueCat module. However, the app does not use it again and deletes the corresponding data before connecting to RevenueCat. We delete at RevenueCat that identifier and the whole purchase history linked to it, receipt included, and confirm it to you. Erasure does not affect the purchase itself, which Apple or Google keep: if you restore it later, a new record is created. Sales reports are kept for the period required by law.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: the CNIL in France, or the AEPD in Spain, where the controller is established.

Security

Traffic between the app and RevenueCat is encrypted (HTTPS). The data stored on your phone sits in the space reserved for the app, which other apps cannot access; it is also protected by your phone's encryption and lock, depending on how your phone is set up.

Changes

The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page. If a new version of the app changes the data processed, this policy is updated before that version is released.
