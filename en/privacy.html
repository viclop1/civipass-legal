Civipass — Privacy policy

Last updated: [publication date] · Version française (reference text)

In short

Civipass works without an account, without ads and without analytics tools. Your answers and your progress are stored on your phone; if your phone's backups are turned on, they may be included in them. The only data the app sends directly to Civipass or its providers concerns the purchase of the full version, and only from the moment you open the purchase screen: it is used to offer you that purchase, to verify it and to restore it.

Who is responsible for your data

The controller is Víctor López Rodríguez, an independent developer based in Madrid (Spain). For any question about your data: civipassapp@gmail.com.

Data stored locally

The app stores the following locally, on your device:

  • your answers to the questions and your progress by topic;
  • the results of your mock exams;
  • your settings: the procedure you are preparing for (multi-year residence permit, residence card or naturalisation) and the language of the explanations;
  • the fact that you bought the full version, so that the app also works offline;
  • the random identifier used for your purchase (see "Buying the full version"), which you can see and copy in the app's Settings, under "Support identifier".

This information is stored on your phone. Apart from the random identifier, which RevenueCat receives if you open the purchase screen (see below), the app does not send it directly to Civipass or to RevenueCat, and we have no access to it; some of it may, however, be included in backups managed by Apple or Google (see below). The procedure you are preparing for may say something about your administrative status: that is why it is never sent to us. This information is deleted from your phone when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

If you have turned on your phone's backups (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your answers, results and settings may be included in them, like the data of your other apps. Those backups are managed by Apple or Google and linked to your account; we have no access to them. Uninstalling Civipass does not delete backups already made: you can manage them from your Apple or Google account.

The app stores its random identifier, the local purchase confirmation and the fact that a purchase is awaiting confirmation in a file excluded from backups. On iPhone, the RevenueCat module built into the app also keeps a copy of this identifier and the purchase status in the app's internal settings, which may be included in iCloud or computer backups. When the app starts without its own identifier, including after a backup has been restored, it deletes the data stored by RevenueCat before connecting to that service and does not use it again. On a new device, after reinstalling or restoring a backup, the app creates a new identifier. You can recover the full version using the "Restore purchase" button.

Buying the full version

Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on your phone. They are responsible for it under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never receive your name, your email address or your payment details.

To verify the purchase, unlock the full version and restore it on another device, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), a technical provider for in-app purchases, acting on our behalf as a processor.

The app only connects to RevenueCat when it needs to: when you open the full version's purchase screen or tap "Restore purchase"; then, if you bought, each time it opens, to verify the purchase; and, if a purchase is awaiting confirmation (a parent's approval, for instance), each time it opens for seven days at most. If you only use the free part without opening that screen, RevenueCat receives nothing. When the app connects, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created by the app, which does not directly contain your name or contact details;
  • if you buy, the purchase receipt sent by the store (product, price, currency, date, store country and transaction number) and the data derived from it: purchase status and access unlocked;
  • the technical data of the connection: IP address, device model, operating system and its version, app version, device language and store country;
  • on iPhone and iPad, the identifier iOS gives to apps from the same developer on your device (the "identifier for vendor"), which the RevenueCat module adds to each connection.

This processing is necessary to perform the purchase contract or, when you open the purchase screen without buying, to take the steps you asked for before entering into it: showing you the offer and handling the purchase attempt (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Apple and Google also send us sales reports (order number, product, price, country and date). We keep them to meet our accounting and tax obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Transfers outside the European Union

RevenueCat processes the data in the United States. This transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914), included in RevenueCat's data processing addendum. Apple and Google may also process your data outside the Union, under their own policies.

How long we keep your data

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • At RevenueCat, if you bought: for as long as Civipass is offered, because the purchase is permanent and must be restorable at any time, unless you ask for it to be erased (see "Your rights"). When our contract with RevenueCat ends, we will ask it, within 30 days of that end, to return or delete this data, as its data processing addendum provides; that deletion remains subject to its usual backup and archiving practices.
  • At RevenueCat, if you opened the purchase screen without buying: the random identifier and the technical data of the connection are deleted automatically no later than 30 days after the app last connected to RevenueCat. This short period leaves time to handle a pending purchase or a payment problem.
  • Requests about your data: while we handle them, then five years from their closing (see "Handling your requests").
  • Sales reports: we keep the supporting records needed for our accounting and tax obligations for the applicable legal period: six years from the last entry made in the accounting books (Article 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code).

What we do not do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data for advertising.
  • No profiling or automated decisions.
  • The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Civipass is meant for adults preparing for the civic exam.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict its processing or to receive it in a portable format, and, in the cases provided for by the GDPR (Article 21), you can object to its processing. Write to civipassapp@gmail.com.

We do not know who you are. For us to find the data that concerns you, give us one of these:

  • the app's support identifier: in Civipass, open Settings and tap "Support identifier" to copy it;
  • if you bought the full version, the order number shown on the Apple or Google receipt.

You can also give us any other information that identifies it. Without any of these, we will generally not be able to find data about you (Article 11 GDPR). They are used to find your data; if we have reasonable doubts that the request comes from you, we may ask for the additional information needed to resolve them, and only that (Article 12(6) GDPR).

We reply within one month of receiving your request. If the request is complex or we receive many, this period can be extended by up to two months; we will tell you so before the first month ends, with the reason and the new deadline (Article 12(3) GDPR).

Erasure. You can ask for erasure at any time. Before writing to us, copy the support identifier: it disappears when you uninstall the app. Then, so that no new record is created while we handle your request, we recommend uninstalling Civipass and not using it again until we have confirmed the erasure: if you bought, or a purchase is pending, it connects to RevenueCat each time it opens. Uninstalling deletes the data stored on your phone, subject to backups already made, but not the data held at RevenueCat: we are the ones who delete it. A backup restored later may contain a copy of the identifier kept by the RevenueCat module. However, the app does not use it again and deletes the corresponding data before connecting to RevenueCat. We delete from RevenueCat that identifier and all the data linked to it, purchase history and receipt included, and confirm it to you. Erasure does not affect the purchase itself, which Apple or Google keep: if you restore it later, a new record is created. Sales reports are kept for the period required by law.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: the CNIL in France, or the AEPD in Spain, where the controller is established.

Handling your requests

When you exercise your rights, we keep your message and our reply (and therefore your email address), the right exercised, the last characters of what allowed us to find your data, the dates and the actions taken. We use them to receive your request, answer it and follow it up, which the GDPR requires of us (Article 6(1)(c), read with Articles 12 to 22 GDPR). After the request is closed, we keep them for five years, in a space with restricted access, only to be able to show that it was handled properly and to defend ourselves against a complaint: this retention is based on our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The five-year period is a prudent choice: the GDPR does not set one. [mailbox to confirm]

Security

Traffic between the app and RevenueCat is encrypted (HTTPS). The data stored on your phone sits in the space reserved for the app, which other apps cannot access; it is also protected by your phone's encryption and lock, depending on how your phone is set up.
