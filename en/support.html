Civipass — Help and contact

Contact: civipassapp@gmail.com · Français

Civipass is made by one person. Every message is read by the person who wrote the app. Expect a reply within a few days.

Getting help

Write to civipassapp@gmail.com. Tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My purchase does not show

On the home screen, tap "Unlock everything", then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple or Google account as when you bought it. If that does not work, write to us with the order number from the Apple or Google receipt.

Asking for a refund

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, and only they can refund them: on iPhone, at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, from your Google Play order history.

Changing your application type or the language of the explanations

Open the app's Settings. The questions always stay in French, as on the day of the exam.

Deleting your Civipass data

Civipass has no account, so there is no account to delete.

  • Your progress, results and settings are stored only on your phone. To erase them, uninstall Civipass or clear its data in your phone's settings.
  • The record of your purchase (a random identifier created by the app and the purchase history, receipt included) is kept by RevenueCat, our purchase provider. To erase it, uninstall Civipass first, then write to civipassapp@gmail.com with the order number shown on the Apple or Google receipt (or any other information that identifies it). We delete it and confirm by email within one month, which can be extended by two months for a complex request. You can still restore the purchase later, because Apple or Google keeps proof of it.
  • What we keep: the sales reports sent by Apple and Google (order number, product, price, country and date), for six years from the last accounting entry of the year, as Spanish accounting law requires.

All the details are in the privacy policy.

Civipass and the French administration

Civipass is not a government service and is not affiliated with the French Ministry of the Interior. The knowledge questions are taken from the official list published by the Ministry at formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr; the situation questions and the explanations are written by Civipass. To register for the civic exam, see that official website.
