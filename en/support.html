Okavi — Help and contact

Contact: okaviapp@gmail.com · Français

Okavi is made by one person. Every message is read by the person who wrote the app. Expect a reply within a few days.

Getting help

Write to okaviapp@gmail.com. Tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My purchase does not show

On the home screen, tap "Unlock everything", then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple or Google account as when you bought it. If your purchase was pending (a parent's approval, a payment in progress), it shows as soon as it is confirmed, when you open the purchase screen again. If that does not work, write to us with the order number from the Apple or Google receipt.

Asking for a refund

On iPhone, refunds go through Apple: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, ask from your Google Play order history, or write to us with the order number: we can also refund it through Google Play.

Changing the procedure you are preparing for or the language of the explanations

Open the app's Settings. The questions always stay in French, as on the day of the exam.

Deleting your Okavi data

Okavi has no account, so there is no account to delete.

  • Your progress, results and settings are stored locally, on your phone. To erase them, uninstall Okavi or clear its data in your phone's settings. iCloud or Google backups already made are managed from your Apple or Google account.
  • Nothing with us or with a provider: the app sends no data to Okavi. The purchase is made directly with Apple or Google, who keep it in your account under their own rules. If you have written to us, or want to know what we keep, write to okaviapp@gmail.com (with the order number from the Google Play receipt if you bought on Android). We reply within one month of receiving your request; if the request is complex or we receive many, this period can be extended by up to two months, and we will tell you so before the first month ends.
  • What we keep: Apple's sales reports (totals per product and country) and Google's monthly totals, with no line per purchase, for six years from the last entry made in the accounting books, as Spanish law requires; and your request and our reply, for five years after it is closed, so that we can show it was handled.

All the details are in the privacy policy.

Okavi and the French administration
