Civipass — Help and contact

Contact: civipassapp@gmail.com · Français

Civipass is made by one person. Every message is read by the person who wrote the app. Expect a reply within a few days.

Getting help

Write to civipassapp@gmail.com. Tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My purchase does not show

On the home screen, tap "Unlock everything", then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple or Google account as when you bought it. If that does not work, write to us with the order number from the Apple or Google receipt.

Asking for a refund

On iPhone, refunds go through Apple: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, ask from your Google Play order history, or write to us with the order number: we can also refund it through Google Play.

Changing the procedure you are preparing for or the language of the explanations

Open the app's Settings. The questions always stay in French, as on the day of the exam.

Deleting your Civipass data

Civipass has no account, so there is no account to delete.

  • Your progress, results and settings are stored locally, on your phone. To erase them, uninstall Civipass or clear its data in your phone's settings. iCloud or Google backups already made are managed from your Apple or Google account.
  • The record at RevenueCat, our technical provider for in-app purchases, only exists if you opened the purchase screen or restored a purchase: a random identifier created by the app and, if you bought, the purchase history, receipt included. If you did not buy, it is deleted automatically no later than 30 days after the app last connected to RevenueCat. To erase it, write to civipassapp@gmail.com with the support identifier (in Civipass, open Settings and tap "Support identifier" to copy it) or, if you bought, the order number shown on the Apple or Google receipt. Copy the identifier before uninstalling: it disappears with the app. Then, so that no new record is created while we handle your request, we recommend uninstalling Civipass and not using it again until we confirm. Uninstalling does not by itself erase that record: we are the ones who delete it. We delete it and confirm by email. We reply within one month of receiving your request. If the request is complex or we receive many, this period can be extended by up to two months; we will tell you so before the first month ends. Erasure does not affect the purchase itself, which Apple or Google keep: if you restore it later, a new record is created.
  • What we keep: the sales reports sent by Apple and Google (order number, product, price, country and date), for six years from the last entry made in the accounting books, as Spanish law requires; and your request and our reply, for five years after it is closed, so that we can show it was handled.

All the details are in the privacy policy.
